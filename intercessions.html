Your privacy and your backup

Important: The shared-screen button only conceals displayed details; it does not encrypt them. Saved prayer details are stored unencrypted in this browser’s local storage, not in a FaithHub server. Other people using this browser profile or device may be able to see them. Clearing browser data can permanently remove them; there is no cross-device sync.

Backup files contain what you've entered, including potentially sensitive information. Keep them somewhere secure and don't share them casually. An import requires confirmation. No automatic emails or sharing.

Growing beyond the screen

Jesus teaches prayer and also calls his followers to love their neighbour. James 2:14–17 reminds us that practical care matters. Prayer cannot replace consent, safeguarding, medical care or the hard work of reconciliation.
