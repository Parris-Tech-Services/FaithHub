How links between these websites work

FaithHub sends visitors to each artist's official material, with a direct link and clear attribution. Links from their websites back to FaithHub would require those site owners' approval and webmaster access; these links have not been arranged or claimed.

To expand later: request permission for an approved artist profile, licensed imagery, official embeds, a shared Psalm reading playlist or reciprocal links. Don't copy their songs, images, lyrics, biographies or subscriber content without permission.
