03 / Honest prayer

Pray the Psalms

The Psalms contain gratitude, protest, confession, trust and silence. You do not need to make a lament end happily. Their poetic language can become a faithful starting point for your own prayer.

Approach slowly: read aloud; notice an image or question; tell God what is true; leave space; return to the whole Psalm in context.
