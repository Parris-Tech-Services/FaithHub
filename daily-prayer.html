A moment with Jesus

What moment are you in?

Read the Scripture

Stay quietly for a moment if you want. When you're ready: “Jesus, help me carry your love into what I do next.”

Pray with your whole self

Feel your feet on the floor. Let your shoulders settle. Sit, stand, open your hands or kneel if comfortable. Breathe naturally. Your posture is an invitation, never a test of sincerity.

If attention wanders, softly return to “Jesus, I am here.” You don't need to force a feeling.

Start with one small anchor

Make room in a day you already live

Choose an existing moment. Leave a small visual cue there: a cross beside the kettle, a Scripture card on your desk, or “Jesus, be with me” on your lock screen.

Add a daily reminder

This website cannot reliably notify you when it is closed. Use a repeating phone alarm labelled “Pause with Jesus — one thank you”, or add the calendar event below.

Import the downloaded .ics file into your calendar, then check its time and notification settings. Downloading alone does not activate a reminder. Import once to avoid duplicates; remove the recurring event in your calendar to stop it.

If you miss your anchor, simply begin again when you notice. Add another only when it helps.

Abide, then love

Let prayer become a way of living

Read Galatians 5:22–23. Choose a fruit to ask the Holy Spirit to grow in you, then try one small act of love.

These are invitations for reflection. No app can measure holiness or tell you how pleased God is with you.

Before you go

“Jesus, thank you for meeting me here. Forgive what needs forgiving. Grow your love in me, and show me my next faithful step.”

After trying a practice, remember your reflection: What helped me turn towards Jesus? What was difficult? What will I try next? You can answer in your own journal or FaithHub's prayer notes.
