# Luke 11:5–13 (NIV) — Ask, seek, knock

**Read the full passage:** [Luke 11:5–13, New International Version](https://bible.com/bible/111/luk.11.5-13.NIV)

**Theme:** Jesus invites us to approach God persistently and with confidence in the Father's goodness. The passage moves from a neighbour asking for bread at midnight, to asking, seeking and knocking, to a father giving good gifts to his children. Jesus closes by naming the gift of the **Holy Spirit**.

## Notice

- **The midnight visitor (verses 5–8):** We may come with real needs, even when it feels inconvenient or vulnerable to ask.
- **Ask, seek, knock (verses 9–10):** Prayer is a continuing relationship with God, not a test of whether we can find perfect words.
- **The good Father (verses 11–13):** Jesus invites us to trust God's character. The climax is not a promise that every request will be answered in exactly the way we imagine, but the Father's generosity in giving the Holy Spirit.

## A gentle practice

Name one need honestly before God. Ask for his help and the Holy Spirit's presence, without needing to manufacture certainty or urgency. Notice where trust, courage, patience or peace might be growing.

## Prayer

Father, I come to you as I am, with my needs and the needs of those I love. Teach me to ask, seek and knock without fear of being a burden. Give me your Holy Spirit, help me trust your goodness, and make me patient, loving and faithful today. Amen.
