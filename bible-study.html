Privacy and recovery

Browser local storage is not encrypted, and someone who uses this browser profile may be able to read your saved notes. This public GitHub Pages site has no account or cloud sync. Back up the exported JSON in your own secure location; opening it in a text editor reveals its contents. Clearing browser data removes saved notes.
