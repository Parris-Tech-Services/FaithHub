About the materials and connections

Private coursework stays private. Cornerstone handbooks, teacher outlines, email attachments, participant details and Drive documents are not uploaded or copied into this public website. Their access and reproduction rights have not been established.

Existing LMS records remain authoritative. The public Cornerstone course portal is linked directly. There is currently no verified API, SSO, cross-site assignment submission or shared progress connection between it and FaithHub. These interactive assignments are original FaithHub companion exercises.

Listening is deliberate. Podcasts and videos open only after you choose a link. They may have their own logins, cookies, adverts and playback rules. Never put confidential family or community information into public reflections.
